# ADR 0014: Canonical Vehicle Engineering Domain

## Status

Accepted for the local preview on 2026-08-21.

## Context

Motorcycle, scooter, EV, and three-wheel concept studies need coupled package,
mass, hardpoint, suspension, braking, road-load, and stability inputs. Treating
these values as loose assembly bodies would make results untraceable, mix SI
and display units, and allow visual geometry to imply a level of engineering
qualification that the project does not provide.

## Decision

1. Add a canonical `vehicle` domain to the semantic project instead of storing
   vehicle intent in generic Assembly bodies.
2. Use original, project-owned generic templates only. Do not ingest PartMode,
   OEM, supplier, or proprietary CAD source or geometry.
3. Store all calculation values in SI units and convert only at UI boundaries.
   Use X forward, Y left, Z up for hardpoints and generated scenes.
4. Keep calculation inputs explicit and versioned. Derive geometry, results,
   warnings, and display formatting from canonical intent.
5. Use bounded transparent equations for trail, quasi-static load transfer,
   wheel rate/frequency, separate hydraulic brake circuits, stopping distance,
   road load, traction, two-wheel lean reference, and rigid three-wheel support
   reactions.
6. Model three-wheel stability with barycentric reactions on the triangular
   tyre-contact polygon. Do not substitute a four-wheel static-stability-factor
   claim. Fail closed when geometry is degenerate or a normal reaction is
   negative.
7. Represent full-droop, design, and full-bump as deterministic chassis-fixed
   hardpoint states generated by one topology solver. Preserve fork offset,
   rigid rear-arm/unit-swing geometry, and tadpole control-arm/upright lengths;
   fail closed on an unreachable state. Treat the result as a kinematic preview,
   not multibody dynamics.
8. Expose one read-only MCP analysis tool with explicit false construction,
   regulatory, and roadworthiness labels. Project mutations remain revisioned
   operations.
9. Give tadpole layouts explicit left/right wishbone, upright, kingpin,
   steering-arm, rack, and tie-rod packaging hardpoints plus ideal inner/outer
   Ackermann targets. Mark rack-travel/constant-tie-rod steering closure,
   bump-steer optimization, parking-brake evaluation, structural validation,
   homologation, detailed tyre behaviour, and transient dynamics unavailable
   rather than estimating them silently.
10. Keep one hardpoint/member graph authoritative for 3D, orthographic
    projection, result calculation, selection, bounds, and verification. Never
    validate a topology from appearance alone.

## Consequences

- Templates, parameters, layers, hardpoints, results, commands, persistence,
  validation, UI, and MCP share one deterministic model.
- A user can understand and change every assumption behind a result.
- The layerable skeleton is useful for early package and geometry discussion.
- Generated geometry and calculations remain visibly unsuitable for
  fabrication, road use, regulatory submission, or safety approval.
- Advanced suspension kinematics, FEA, tyre, controls, crash, thermal,
  durability, and homologation programs require separate qualified solvers,
  source data, and evidence.
